The Great Pencil Duel Meera and Kavya had been best friends since Grade 5. They shared a desk, a lunchbox and almost every secret.One Monday morning, Meera opened her pencil box and gasped. Her favourite blue pencil, the one with a tiny golden star near the eraser, was missing.

"Kavya, did you take it?" she asked.

"Why would I?" said Kavya. "I have my own red pencil."

"Then why is there blue lead on your notebook?"

Kavya stared at the smudge. "That's not mine!" she said, and grabbed Meera's pencil box to check. Meera pulled it back. In the tug-of-war, two pencils flew out, and soon the two friends were waving their pencils like swords.

"Take that!" cried Meera, tapping Kavya's pencil.

"Defend yourself!" laughed Kavya, tapping back.

Crack! Kavya's red pencil snapped in half. Both girls froze.

Then something rolled out from under Meera's own book and stopped at their feet: the blue pencil with the golden star. It had slipped under the book on Friday.

Meera went red. "I'm so sorry. I blamed you for nothing."

Kavya grinned and held up the broken red pencil. "Then you owe me a new one."

They shared the blue pencil that day, one writing while the other rested. At lunch, Meera bought Kavya a new red pencil, and they promised to ask before accusing.
